Support

If something goes wrong, keep an independent original and contact LILI at the address below. Send only the information needed to investigate.

Contactrayfra@qq.com ↗LILI

What to include in a report

Include the app version, OS version, device, camera model, RAW compression mode, steps and error message. Photos and logs are optional. Remove unnecessary people, locations and other personal information before sending. We will never ask for your Apple Account password or payment verification codes.

A photo will not import

One extension may contain different cameras or compression modes. Try selecting the original in Files. For iCloud originals, check connectivity and available storage. Keep your original and do not overwrite it while troubleshooting.

Saving to Photos fails

Check the permission to add photos in system settings. Refusing permission still allows editing, file export and system sharing. Confirm that saving has actually finished before managing other copies.

Do exports include location

Export defaults retain shooting metadata and may include GPS from the original. Turn off location retention or remove shooting metadata before sharing if needed. Saved export preferences affect future exports.

Originals and backups

Import creates a local app copy; export creates a new file. Keep independent backups. Clearing preview caches does not delete edits. Deleting app copies does not recall shared files or remove copies in Photos, file providers or system backups.
