Photos and local processing

The app copies the photos you select for local editing. It reads image content, filenames, camera and lens details, capture time, location and other embedded metadata as needed. Edits, presets, preferences and previews stay in app storage. Device memory and thermal state help manage processing resources.

Network and third parties

Photo editing and composition recommendations run on your device. We do not upload your photos for AI analysis or training. This version has no accounts, advertising or tracking SDKs, and online camera configuration updates are not enabled. iCloud downloads, backups, file providers, sharing destinations and websites you open may use networks under their own policies.

Purchases and trial access

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app verifies App Store transaction records, including product identifiers, purchase dates and revocation status, to manage trial and lifetime access. These records remain on the device through StoreKit; we do not receive your payment card details or upload your transaction records to our server. A last-observed time is stored in the device Keychain to reduce trial clock rollback and may remain after reinstalling. Store access and restoration may use Apple’s network and Apple Account under Apple’s policies.

Permissions and choices

Import uses system photo and file pickers, without requiring access to your entire photo library. Saving to Photos requests add-only permission; refusal leaves editing, file export and sharing available. This version does not request camera, microphone, contacts, live location or tracking permission. Change permissions in system settings.

Location in exported photos

By default, exported copies retain shooting metadata and, when present and supported by the format, the original shooting location. Before sharing, turn off “Keep shooting location” or remove shooting metadata in export settings if you do not want to disclose it. Saved export preferences affect later exports. This does not obtain your live location.

Storage and deletion

Local originals and edits remain until you delete the local copy or remove app data. Clearing preview caches does not delete originals or edits. Export temporary files may remain until app or system cleanup. Copies saved elsewhere and system backups must be managed separately; deleting inside the app does not recall shared files. We have no photo recovery server.

Support and retention

If you email us, we use your email address, message and optional attachments to answer and troubleshoot. Send only necessary information. QQ Mail and your email provider handle delivery under their own policies, possibly across regions. We retain support records only as needed to resolve the request, meet legal duties or handle disputes, then delete them. Apple handles its own store and system data under its policies.

Your information rights

Contact rayfra@qq.com to request access, a copy, correction, deletion, explanation or withdrawal of consent where applicable. We verify only what is necessary and respond within applicable legal time limits. We cannot remotely access or erase data held only on your device. You may also complain to the competent authority; mandatory local rights remain available.

Children, security and updates

The app is not directed at children. Minors should use it with a guardian; obtain required guardian consent before sending personal information. We use appropriate security measures but cannot guarantee absolute security. Material changes will be explained in the app and any legally required consent obtained separately. New processing or cross-border transfers require the applicable safeguards; continued use alone is not consent.
